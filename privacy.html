FazPost - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

FazPost is a personal tool for uploading the owner's own videos to TikTok as drafts and viewing the owner's own TikTok videos and statistics. This policy explains what data it handles.

1. Data we handle

  • TikTok login (Login Kit): basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) to confirm which account is connected.
  • Videos you upload (Content Posting API): video files you choose are sent to TikTok as drafts in your account.
  • Your video list and statistics (Display API): titles, thumbnails and public counts such as views, likes, comments and shares for your own videos, shown only to you inside the App.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used only to perform the upload.

2. How we use it

Only to upload the videos you select to your TikTok drafts and to show you your own video list and statistics. The App does not publish videos; you publish them yourself in TikTok.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored securely and not shared. Video files are not kept longer than needed to complete the upload.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, except TikTok as needed to perform the upload, or where required by law.

5. Your choices and deletion

Disconnect at any time via TikTok Settings > Security & permissions > Apps. To request deletion of any stored data, email us and it will be deleted within 30 days.
