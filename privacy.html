Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Faz AI Creator ("the App", "we") handles your data when you connect your TikTok account.

1. Data we collect

  • TikTok profile (via Login Kit): your display name, avatar and TikTok open ID.
  • Video information (via Display API): your video list and public statistics such as views and likes, shown in your dashboard.
  • Content you publish (via Content Posting API): videos and captions you choose to upload or post to your account.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok so the App can act on your behalf.

2. How we use it

Only to provide the App's features: showing your profile and video performance, and posting content you have approved. Nothing is posted without your explicit confirmation.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored securely and encrypted. We keep data only as long as your account is connected.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not share it with third parties except service providers needed to run the App, and as required by law. Content you ask the AI to process may be sent to an AI provider to generate results.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect at any time in the App or under TikTok Settings > Security & permissions > Apps. To have your data deleted, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
